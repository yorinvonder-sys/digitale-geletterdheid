## 4. Evidence Rules

- Browse official sources for any current legal deadline, regulator position, or provider claim.
- Link the exact source used when writing or approving school-facing claims.
- Do not rely on unofficial AI Act summary sites as primary authority. Use them only for orientation, then verify against EUR-Lex or official guidance.
- Treat provider privacy pages, DPA terms, subprocessor pages, and region/retention settings as evidence that can drift.
- Keep quoted legal text short; summarize and cite rather than copying long passages.
